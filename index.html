Scenarios

Build a solar system. See if it lasts.

Tap the map to drop a planet on a circular orbit. Hold and slide to position it.

Drag back from a planet, like a slingshot, to fling it onto a different orbit. The dotted line is its predicted path. It ignores the other planets, so it is often wrong.

Launch to run 10,000 years. Harmony rewards survivors, time in the green habitable zone and orbital rhythms. Mayhem rewards ejections, collisions and planets fed to the star.

A rocky or ocean world that stays in the green zone grows life: microbes, plants, animals, cities, then radio signals. Short trips outside are survivable; leaving for long wipes it out.

Bigger planets score more and bully their neighbours.

Every planet plays a note set by its orbit. Orbits in a simple rhythm, like 2:1 or 3:2, sound in tune.
